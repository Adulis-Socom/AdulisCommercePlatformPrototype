## Specification Summary

The Adulis prototype covers five product surfaces:
1. Company Website
2. Seller Storefront
3. Mobile App (Buyer, Seller Companion, Courier)
4. Seller Console
5. Operator Console

With 12 end-to-end demo flows (A–L), a complete design system, realistic demo data, and strict product rules (no payment confirmation by Seller, no government ID verification, no live GPS, Courier QR handoff flow, etc.).
